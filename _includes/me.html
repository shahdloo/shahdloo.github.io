I am an Applied Scientist in Microsoft's Applied Sciences Group. I develop multimodal agentic systems optimized for efficient on-device inference on Windows.

Previously, I developed machine learning models for markerless motion capture at Vicon and worked on functional MRI acquisition and reconstruction under subject motion at the University of Oxford's FMRIB Centre. My MRI research has explored reconstruction methods combining deep learning, machine learning, and signal processing. My research in computational neuroscience has examined how the brain represents visual and spoken information under natural stimulation conditions by studying fMRI responses using various machine learning and deep learning methods.

I studied electrical engineering at Amirkabir University and Sharif University in Tehran. After developing industrial embedded systems in the energy sector for a couple of years, I moved to Bilkent University in Ankara for graduate research in MRI and computational neuroscience with Tolga Çukur.
