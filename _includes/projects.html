• Latent-Lens: visual perception in small language models
    A small language model can use visual information from a vision-language model through continuous prefix embeddings. Latent-Lens projects the sender's hidden states into the receiver's embedding space, enabling visual question answering and image captioning. Accepted at NeurIPS 2026.

  • Markerless human pose estimation and tracking
    At Vicon, I developed machine learning models that estimate and track human pose directly from video. This approach supports motion capture without attached markers or special clothing.

  • Motion correction for awake non-human primate fMRI
    Movement changes the magnetic field during MRI, making functional imaging of awake monkeys particularly challenging. We developed acquisition and reconstruction methods that correct these changes and improve accelerated fMRI.

  • Attention and action representations in the brain
    We mapped how hundreds of objects and actions are represented across the cortex, then examined how these representations change when people search for particular action categories.
    Paper · Brain viewer

  • Language, context, and attention in the brain
    Using neural language models and brain recordings during story listening, we studied how attention changes the timescales over which the brain integrates context.
    Paper

  • Rapid self-tuning compressed sensing MRI
    Compressed-sensing MRI reconstruction depends on regularization parameters that vary across subjects and datasets. We developed a self-tuning method that selects these parameters during reconstruction using projections onto epigraph sets of the l1 and total-variation norms.
    Paper

  • Fat suppression and partial-volume effects in bSSFP MRI
    Combining in-phase and out-of-phase acquisitions can suppress fat signals in bSSFP MRI. We studied how the weighting parameter affects fat suppression and partial-volume artifacts, and identified settings for different imaging requirements.
